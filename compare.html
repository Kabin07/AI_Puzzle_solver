Heuristic Comparison

Greedy Best-First Search runs with Manhattan Distance and with Misplaced Tiles on the same puzzle instance, then on a batch of random puzzles. Times are measured without visualisation; very fast searches are repeated and averaged because browser timers are coarse.

How to compare
1
Pick the puzzle
The puzzle you used in the Solver is loaded automatically. Edit it or generate a new one.
2
Choose the batch size
How many random puzzles to average over (in addition to your puzzle).
3
Run Comparison
Read both tables, the four charts and the auto-generated observations. Download the results as CSV if needed.
